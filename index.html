07 FAQ

Good questions.

Anything else? Write to hello@shapelark.com.

How long does it take?

You see a first preview within 2 business days of sending your photos and dimensions. Final delivery then follows the plan: Catalog in 3 business days, Launch in 5 and Hero in 7. For the free sample, we confirm the date when we reply.

What do I need to send?

For the free sample, just a link to your product page or 2–3 clear phone photos, plus its rough size. Not sure what you have? Send what you can and we’ll ask for anything missing.

For a paid order, the more we have, the more exact the result:

  • Photos from a few angles (front, back, side and top), taken in daylight.
  • Height, width and depth. A ruler or tape measure in one photo works too.
  • Your brand colors, if you have them, or a photo of the product next to a sheet of white paper.
  • Your logo and label artwork, if you have the files. If not, we’ll rebuild them from your photos.
How do revisions work?

A revision round is one set of changes, for example “make the cap a little darker and turn the label toward the camera”. Catalog includes 2 rounds; Launch and Hero include 3. You also see a plain gray preview of the shape before colors and materials go on, so any shape issues are caught early.

Which file formats do I get?

Full-resolution PNG and JPEG images, sized for your store, Amazon, Etsy and social, with transparent PNGs on request. Turntables are delivered as MP4 video. The Hero plan also includes the 3D scene file (Blender format), so you have your own copy of the model, materials and lighting.

Can I use the images anywhere?

Yes. You get full commercial usage rights to the delivered images and video, worldwide and with no expiry: your store, marketplaces, ads, social, packaging and print. We only show your product in our portfolio if you give us permission.

Is the free sample really free?

Yes. We render one of your products as one preview-size image, at no cost and with no payment details. If you like it and go ahead, we keep building on the same model.

What kinds of products work best?

Products with clear shapes and real materials: bottles, jars, cosmetics, drinkware, candles, small electronics, accessories and homeware. Soft goods such as clothing are harder; ask us first and we'll tell you honestly.
